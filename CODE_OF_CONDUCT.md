# Code of conduct

Learning Booklet Studio contributors are expected to make collaboration safe, rigorous, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith while still verifying claims.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, access needs, and levels of experience.
- Accept correction, acknowledge uncertainty, and repair mistakes transparently.
- Protect private reports, user data, and unpublished source material.
- Keep technical disagreement proportional to project impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, stalking, doxxing, retaliation, repeated disruption, or publishing another person's private information is not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository host or distributing marketplace's maintainer-contact channel. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Maintainers should recuse themselves from reports in which they are involved, protect confidentiality as far as practical, and apply a proportionate response ranging from a private correction to temporary or permanent exclusion.

Nothing in this policy prevents a person from contacting appropriate emergency, legal, workplace, or platform-safety resources.
